### PR #311 — exact conversion and retained residual

```text
scaled   = q * n
whole    = scaled / d
residual = scaled % d

```

and proves split conservation when residual information is retained.

Qualified boundary:

```text

    !=

    -> exact scaled conservation under splitting
```

Residual is exact information, not merely an implementation error term.

Not earned: `RoundingPolicy`, persistent Residual fact, `ConversionReceipt`, FX-specific arithmetic ontology.

## Cross-observation compression

The seven probes looked different:

```text
overspending
recognition
historical finality
valuation timing
rate-source conflict
fractional conversion
rounding carry placement
```

but repeatedly exposed the same smaller boundaries.

### Projection is not automatically retained truth

Report nouns such as Remaining, recognised expense, realised/unrealised valuation, or rounded per-piece quantity may be derived answers rather than canonical facts.

### Coordinate is not authority

A DateRange, knowledge horizon, or effective valuation coordinate says where a question is asked. It does not necessarily choose among several admissible policies, sources, or presentations.

### Provenance is not authority

```text
where evidence came from
    !=
which evidence should win
```

Likewise:

```text
what exact quantity exists
    !=
where a visible carry should be placed
```

### Candidate queries are cheaper than scalar queries

```text
show all retained candidates
    requires less authority

choose one answer
    may require explicit authority
```

LOAM should avoid collapsing evidence earlier than the question requires.

### Current state is smaller than retained history

Correction history and temporal valuation both show that a current frontier may be insufficient to reproduce historical answers.

### Exactness and presentation are separate

```text
selected exact relation
+ source quantity
    -> exact whole + residual information

exact information
    does not uniquely determine
per-piece integral presentation
```

Rounding presentation should not silently become ontology.
